# Google Play Screenshot Plan

Capture only real screens from the signed preview APK with consent-safe demonstration data. Do not show real emails, phone numbers, leads, tokens, admin accounts or QR codes that resolve to private/test content.

1. **Premium Home / Digital Identity** — approved primary card and clear share-ready state.
2. **Card Preview** — polished card identity and business details.
3. **QR Centre** — approved card selection and QR/share tools.
4. **Leads CRM** — populated but synthetic pipeline and follow-up indicators.
5. **Insights** — useful range/card filters and representative non-sensitive metrics.
6. **Business Tools** — Basic WhatsApp and Quick Messages; do not imply disconnected Cloud API features are active.
7. **Profile & Security** — verified account, privacy/security entries; avoid exposing admin controls unless listing targets that audience.

Use consistent Android device framing and Play-supported portrait dimensions, retain readable text, avoid status-bar notifications, and capture dark/navy areas without crushed contrast. Prepare phone screenshots first; only create tablet assets if tablet support is genuinely tested and intended.
