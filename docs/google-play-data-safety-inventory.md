## Items requiring business confirmation

- Whether data is used for advertising, sold, or “shared” under Google Play definitions.
- Complete provider/subprocessor list, retention periods, backup deletion, encryption at rest and security certifications.
- Whether analytics tokens are linked to identity in deployed data and whether raw IP/user-agent data is retained by hosting/provider logs.
- Final collection optionality, lawful basis, consent/notice and support/deletion response process.
