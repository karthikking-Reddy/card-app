YNS Card helps signed-in users create and share digital business cards, manage enquiries and follow-ups, review card activity, and use enabled business tools.

## Information handled

- Account data: authentication identifier, email, name, provider identity details and platform role.
- Digital-card data: name, job title, company, phone, email, location, biography, services, links, images, card slug, visibility and approval state.
- CRM data: lead/contact details, enquiry content, notes, stages, estimated values, follow-ups and related card identifiers.
- Usage data: card views, contact clicks, saves, shares and privacy-safe visitor identifiers where the public-card implementation records them.
- Integration data: WhatsApp-related configuration, conversations/messages or provider status only where the relevant feature is configured.
- Files: profile/company images and temporary locally generated QR, VCF and CSV files used for user-requested export or sharing.

The source code does not request precise location, device contacts, camera, microphone or push-notification access in the release configuration. Photo access is requested contextually when a user selects a profile image.

## How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, operate digital cards, process public enquiries, provide CRM and analytics features, enforce approval/administration rules, prevent abuse, provide support, and maintain service security.

## Providers and external actions

Supabase provides authentication, database and storage services. Google may provide OAuth authentication when selected. WhatsApp click-to-chat opens an external WhatsApp destination chosen by the user; provider automation is available only when configured. Public cards may contain links supplied by card owners.

Whether these providers are processors or independent controllers, their legal locations, and the complete subprocessor list require business/legal confirmation.

## Sharing and sale

The app sends data to service providers necessary to operate enabled features. The code audit does not establish whether the business “sells” or “shares” data under every applicable privacy law; this requires business/legal confirmation. YNS must not claim an opt-out that is not implemented.

## Security and retention

Network communication uses HTTPS/TLS endpoints. Supabase Row Level Security and authorized RPCs are part of the intended access model. Exact retention schedules, backups, incident procedures and deletion time frames require confirmation.

## User choices and rights

Users can edit or deactivate card information and export certain CRM/insights data. Complete account export and secure account deletion are not yet deployed. Before release, YNS must provide an authenticated in-app deletion request and the required public deletion-request page. Applicable access, correction, deletion or objection requests require a confirmed support channel.

## Public-card visitors

An approved, active and public card may record a card-view event and interaction events. Public enquiry forms may store visitor-supplied name, email, phone and message. Raw IP retention, cookie use, visitor notice/consent, lawful basis and opt-out requirements must be confirmed against the deployed web/backend implementation.

## Children

The intended audience and minimum age require business/legal confirmation. Do not publish a children-directed claim without a product and legal decision.
