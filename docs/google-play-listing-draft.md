## App name

YNS Card

## Short description

Create, approve and share digital business cards, then manage leads and follow-ups.

## Full description

YNS Card brings your professional digital identity and everyday lead workflow into one mobile app.

Create digital business cards with your contact and business information. Submit cards for administrator approval, then share approved public cards through a link, QR code or contact file. Keep enquiries organized with lead stages, notes and follow-ups, and review activity through bounded insights.

Key features:

- Create and manage multiple digital business cards
- Approval-controlled public sharing
- Share links, QR images and VCF contacts
- Organize leads, stages, notes and follow-up tasks
- Export CRM and insights CSV files
- Use basic WhatsApp click-to-chat and saved Quick Messages
- Role-aware administration for authorized organizations
- Sign in with email or Google in an installed app build

Some provider integrations require separate business configuration and are shown as disconnected or setup-required until enabled.
